## Why I built it

La Cafe is a real cloud kitchen, so this one has to work for people who are hungry. It needed a brand, a menu customers could browse, and a way for the kitchen to keep track of what it buys and spends.

## What it does

The first product is the customer menu. It has the dish photos, descriptions, portions, and links to order. We used agents to build it together with the brand, which is why the wordmark, the menu, and the food photos look like they belong to the same café.

The second is for the kitchen. We’re building an internal kitchen OS that tracks supplier purchases, stock and wastage, and monthly expenses. The menu helps you decide what to eat. The kitchen OS helps us decide what to buy and shows us what we’re spending.

The menu is its own app because looking at a menu is a small job. You browse, you pick, you tap through to order. Keeping it separate means we can keep changing how the kitchen tracks purchases and stock without touching the thing customers see.
