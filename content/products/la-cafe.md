## One kitchen, two products

The customer menu brings dish photos, descriptions, portions, and ordering links into one place. We used agents to build it alongside the brand, so the wordmark, menu, and dish imagery feel like parts of the same café.

Behind the counter, we’re building an internal kitchen OS for supplier purchases, stock and wastage, and monthly expenses. The menu helps someone decide what to order. The kitchen OS helps the team decide what to buy and understand what it’s spending.

## Why the menu is a separate app

Browsing a menu is a small task. Customers can look through the dishes and go straight to an ordering channel when they’re ready. Keeping the menu separate also lets us change how the kitchen tracks purchases and stock on its own schedule.
