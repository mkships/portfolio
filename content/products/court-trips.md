## Why I built it

I came back from a tennis vacation and wanted to book the next one straight away. That turned out to be harder than it sounds. The good camps and clinics are scattered across club sites, resort pages, and whatever another player happens to recommend.

## What it does

Court Trips puts camps, clinics, and getaways for tennis, pickleball, and padel side by side so you can compare them. I’m building it with Claude Code.

Once you find a trip you like, I send you to the organizer. They know the dates, the availability, and the booking terms far better than I do. I want Court Trips to be good at one thing, which is helping you find the trip.
