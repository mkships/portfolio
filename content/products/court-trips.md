## How it started

After a tennis vacation, I wanted to find the next one. The answers were scattered across club sites, resort pages, and recommendations from other players.

## Why booking stays with the organizer

I’m building Court Trips with Claude Code so players can compare camps, clinics, and getaways across tennis, pickleball, and padel. I chose to send players to the organizer once they’ve found a good fit. That keeps Court Trips focused on discovery and gives players one place to confirm dates, availability, and booking terms.
