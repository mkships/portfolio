## How I think about the work

I work with Start.dev as a freelance consultant on agent-driven marketing and growth. I’m interested in making repeatable tasks easier to run while keeping the decisions about audience, positioning, and what to test with the person accountable for the work.

The walkthrough uses a fictional product and campaign. It shows the kind of handoff I’m interested in, from source material and a hypothesis through to a draft someone can review and a test someone can measure.
