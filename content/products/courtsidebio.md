## The link a coach can send anywhere

I’m building one profile for independent tennis, pickleball, and padel coaches. A player can see what a coach teaches, when they’re usually available, and how to get in touch, all from a link the coach can share in a social bio or a chat.

## Why the flow starts a conversation

A recurring time window gives the player enough information to ask a specific question. The coach can confirm the details directly over email or WhatsApp. The profile handles the introduction, and the coach keeps control of the schedule.
