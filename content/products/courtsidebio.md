## Why I built it

Independent coaches need one link they can send anywhere. I’m building courtsidebio for tennis, pickleball, and padel coaches who want a player to see what they teach, when they’re usually free, and how to reach them.

## What it does

A coach sets up one profile and drops the link in a social bio or a chat.

I didn’t build a booking calendar. The profile shows a coach’s typical availability, which is enough for a player to ask a specific question, like whether Thursday at five works. The coach replies over email or WhatsApp and sorts out the rest. Coaches already run their schedules their own way, and I didn’t want courtsidebio to get in the middle of that.
