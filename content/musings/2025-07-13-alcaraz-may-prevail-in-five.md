Very hard call - I actually think Sinner’s fast and hard hitting fits very well for grass courts but I think we will have a 5 setter.

Alcaraz may prevail in 5.

Reasons -

I’m no more worried about Alcaraz’s serving. He is almost servebotting a few times.

Alcaraz and Sinner - both are peak returners but Alcaraz would come up with creative ways to finish points which means Sinner’s lockdown mode will keep getting disrupted.

Lockdown mode will only work when you give into that. When you keep finding ways to disrupt it - usually the attacker prevails.

The only concern is we just don’t know which version of Carlos will show up - which means Sinner will simply dominate.
