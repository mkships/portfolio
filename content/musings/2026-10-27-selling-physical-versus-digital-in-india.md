My thesis is - it is slightly easy selling physical businesses to Indians while using digital infra for engagement, lead generation etc. (basically all our Flipkart/Ola/Uber/Swiggy/Zomato).

Purely digital businesses like SaaS/mobile apps is almost tough to monetize here.
