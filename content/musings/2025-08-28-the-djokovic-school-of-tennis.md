Djokovic school of tennis.

People will get mesmerized by looking at Federer and Nadal’s highlights but those two were full talent and artistry.

Not saying Djokovic isn’t talented - his talent lies in being disciplined and doing the right amount of work to win it. Not more, not less. The perfect amount to win.

More and more players will adopt this. We can already see Alcaraz being very consistent now and winning some ugly matches.
