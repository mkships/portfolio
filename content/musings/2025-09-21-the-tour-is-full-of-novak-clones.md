The large reason why Alcaraz is dominating is also more about how he seems to be the only one who is breaking free and plays more creatively, a bit of all court tennis and isn’t scared to be inventive on court.

Rest of the tour including Sinner are essentially Novak clones with only Sinner being the guy who is playing like Novak in 2x mode.

Even the game has become very physical and at academies I see lot more emphasis on fitness, Novak style elastic acrobatic defense. This kinda makes tennis very elite in the physical part but it lost its 80s/90s crafty and elegance on court.
