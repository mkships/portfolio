I think in tennis match ups are more important than form. Carlos has some tactical advantages over Sinner in this matchup.

Sinner now plays somewhat all court game but at its core he is an aggressive baseliner whose movement now has reached prime Big 3 level.

Carlos is a default all court player and has the surprise element and already is a Nadal like mover on court.

Lack of variety for Sinner is the reason I favour Carlos here.

Also Sinner won’t have the same mentality like he did against Rudd. He knows that he is going to win that match. Doesn’t matter 6-0 or 6-4.

But he won’t come feeling invincible against Carlos.
