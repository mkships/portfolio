Sometime back - Arindam shared one of his threads on his fitness journey. Standout insight for me in that - to lose 1 kg, you need to effectively burn 7500+ calories. Avg. Indian meal is ~1000 calorie+ & one cardio workout at gym burns ~300 calories. Simple math for weight loss!
