Nadal from 2005-10: Superhuman stamina, moved like Duracell bunny on clay. He will rip through Alcaraz defense, he will overwhelm with his FH.

It’ll be Nadal in 3 or 4 sets.

If it’s any other version of Nadal - it’ll go to 5 sets. And if it’s 5 sets - Alcaraz can roll a dice on that one.

In both scenarios - I’m assuming a prime Alcaraz.

Small caveat here is that we haven’t yet seen the highest peak of Alcaraz yet. And also even 36 year old Djokovic could overwhelm Alcaraz on clay sometimes.
