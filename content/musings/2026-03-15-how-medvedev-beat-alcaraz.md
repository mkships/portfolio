Watched the highlights. This reminded me a bit of the US Open semifinal they played in 2023.

One of the Achilles heals for Alcaraz is he can sometimes get very fazed when the opponent is just peaking with relentless aggression.

Credit to Medvedev - he played aggressive, was relentless from the back of the court, and surprised Carlos with some impossible retrievals. Alcaraz’s FH was very loopy which gave more time to Medvedev to continue inflict more damage on both wings.

Relentless aggression, defense from those tentacles 🐙 - full credit to Medvedev for showing how to do it.

He beat Sinner as well so many times so let’s see if he can still do it against Sinner in the finals!
