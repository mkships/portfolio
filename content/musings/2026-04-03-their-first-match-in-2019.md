TIL.

In 2019 - Carlos Alcaraz Garfia played his first career challenger match against Jannik Sinner who was on a 16 match winning hot streak and was one of the hot young prospects on circuit.

Alcaraz won this match and became the first player born in 2003 to win a match at Challenger level. He didn’t even have an ATP point/ranking at this time while Jannik was in 300s.

Within 3 years of this match - Alcaraz would go onto win his first grand slam and claim world number 1 ranking. And Jannik Sinner would become his arch rival and both have become the proverbial Big 2 of tennis!
