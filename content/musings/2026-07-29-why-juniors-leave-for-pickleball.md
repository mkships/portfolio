Will put some blame on ATP/WTA as well - they are not doing much to add new events in Asia or India. Tour is disproportionately concentrated in Europe and USA.

I know many top juniors who have moved to pickleball purely for money. Lot of celebrity money is flowing into pickleball and it's only fair that these players want to cash out now.

I think to some extent it is also because we just don't have a breakout star like we had in Sania Mirza or Paes - Bhupati where masses also know their names and looked forward to them. (Nagal came close, but didn't really break out).
