May be a stupid bit of trivia but Carlos Alcaraz is also the only guy to have completed Career Slam exactly in reverse order (and the only one to do it that way male or female)

Starting with US Open in 2022, Wimbledon in 2023 (and 2024), RG in 2024 (and 2025) and AO in 2026.

For everyone else / the order is as random as it gets!
