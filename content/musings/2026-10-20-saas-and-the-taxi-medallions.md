In this analogy, the taxi medallions won over Uber in every lucrative market forcing Uber either to kneel down to their demands or become the by default supply to Uber - in both cases the taxi medallions ensured they’re good and safe despite the existence of Uber.

Even the government had to change laws or tweak things to ensure the survival of taxis.

Many SaaS companies own massive customer data, built B2B associations (similar to how taxi medallions are in cahoots with the local law) and have great branding. Many of them in large markets will survive just like how the taxi medallions did.
