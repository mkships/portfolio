There is a clear methodical and categorical madness to Sinner’s approach towards game and winning. He is determined and obsessed.

Alcaraz is more akin to a child like joy who is just happy to play tennis and keep smiling, while entertaining the fans and the crowd. Still determined but not categorically obsessed.

Usually the first approach plays out better in the long term. We have seen that with Djokovic already!
