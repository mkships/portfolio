It is a very tough debate. Purely on outcomes - Alcaraz is well and truly ahead. He completed the clay masters and won 2 RGs in a row - he has completed the clay court resume.

But having said that Federer has made 4 RG finals in a row. He didn’t win Montecarlo but made finals 3x in a row. He didn’t win Rome but had match points in 2006 (easily the best non slam match in the last 30 years). Federer defeated a prime and peaking Djokovic in 2011. Even after skipping RG for 4 years, he still made semifinals in 2019 beating Wawrinka (ex champion) and Ruud (future finalist 2X) en route.

In all these times - he was only stopped by Nadal - an unprecedented 14 time champ.

So if we think with some nuance - they’re basically both great clay court players with one having better outcomes. But imo - talent and clay court prowess wise - they rank very much same for me.
