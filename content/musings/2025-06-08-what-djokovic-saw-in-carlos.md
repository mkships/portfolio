After losing 2023 Wimbledon to Alcaraz - Novak Djokovic in press mentioned that he saw the best attributes of big 3 in Carlos’s game which was in full display today.

- he got the fantastic, all out aggression tennis and shotmaking from Federer

- he got the endurance and foot speed of Nadal and a bit of that heavy FH.

- he got the mental resilience of Novak himself.

The match he played today at @rolandgarros is the best example and is a full @carlosalcaraz experience for everyone!
