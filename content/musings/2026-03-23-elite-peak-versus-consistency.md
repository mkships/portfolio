Carlos has a very elite peak while Sinner is the more consistent player.

It’s simply boils down to their play styles. Not even to do anything about matchups.

Carlos tries to be creative on every shot, always tries to go for a winner, surprise the opponent and has some penchant to be a crowd pleaser. This has some risk attached to it. But when it works, he will look like greatest player in history.

Sinner simply plays his shots with discipline and doesn’t do anything that’s not necessary. With his ultra-hard mode hitting from the baseline, this is enough to beat just about any player. But his inability to not do that “extra” is why he will never have a peak like Alcaraz.
