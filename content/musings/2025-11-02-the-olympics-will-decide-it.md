Olympics will be the biggest deciding tournament. Alcaraz missed it by a whisker - he will not get a clay court Olympics final again. Next one is in LA - hard courts that may favour Sinner.

Apart from that Sinner doesn’t have any perceived weakness for any one of the above tournaments while Alcaraz does seem to struggle with this Shanghai - Paris stretch.

My bet is on Sinner to do it first but I won’t be surprised if Alcaraz surprises me (because he keeps doing it).

Would guess, career slam to be the first priority for both. And remember even there Alcaraz hasn’t made a SF also at AO while Sinner had championship points already at RG.
