He is being modest here. Djokovic’s dropshot is extremely predictable - because he always places it in the exact same spot once he locks the player in the deep corner of ad court.

Carlos hits the most unpredictable dropshot imo. He doesn’t have any signature spot and he is even more special to hit a FH dropshot which I’ve not seen most pros do.
