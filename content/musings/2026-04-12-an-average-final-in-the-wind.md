I felt both guys played a pretty average match because of the windy conditions. Sinner as usual whacked the ball pretty hard - not like Alcaraz didn’t have his chances but you can see that he didn’t have the same confidence of last year.

Serve was totally off for Carlos, overall Sinner looked and played better. Deserved the win.

Contrary to what the H2H says, these guys always had very tight matches - it’s always 50.1 to 49.9 kinda odds.

Sinner looking ultra good again. Would be historic to have two guys finishing career slam in the same year one after another.
