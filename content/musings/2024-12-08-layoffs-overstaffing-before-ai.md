Problem is not just AI but the overstaffing that was done during the 2021 tech bubble. Even salaries were hiked in an unsustainable way. Layoffs have already happened of course but the salary rationalisation hasn’t happened and that’s why orgs probably continue with layoffs.

As per AI productivity - yes it’s there but I don’t think we have seen it in scale yet.
