@jmgmoron Sinner’s game is basically very close to 2011 aggressive Djokovic. May be even more aggressive.

And what does aggressive Djokovic doesn’t like at all? Windy conditions.

Wind is perfect for Carlos style play on clay because he is least affected by it as he is used to hitting the ball with big margin and with lot more weight.

Big margin implies - less unforced errors and more weight means Sinner can never attack these balls with the precision that he usually does.

If the situation is normal - I think Sinner is looking lot more confident!
