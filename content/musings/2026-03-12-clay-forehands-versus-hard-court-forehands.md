I somewhat agree with that analysis, I’m only a rec player but there is a difference in how I hit my forehands on a clay court vs hard court.

Clay - more spin, more clearance. Pace is not an issue because the clay takes away the pace of the ball anyways.

Hard - more pace, flatter, allow the ball to skid on the synthetic - Novak uses this very effectively. But playing like this is futile on clay.

Alcaraz imo effortlessly switches between a flat FH and a high topspin FH. I’ve not seen Sinner do it so much tbh.
