Many casual tennis fans do a lame comparison of Carlos with Nadal (as both are from Spain, and also happen to be great on clay) or with Novak (as both Carlos and Novak show great clutch mentality when they are down).

But it is clear as day where Carlos's tennis inspiration (actual shot-making, match IQ etc.) comes from. He in fact is as aggressive and goes for first strike winners just as Federer and also has that same creativity to always come up with lot of variety, again like Federer.
