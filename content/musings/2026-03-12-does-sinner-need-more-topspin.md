Indian Wells is also a very slow, high bounce hard court and with the desert wind - it mimics a clay court conditions - so this is the best place to test a very high topspin FH.

But Sinner is naturally gifted with a great racket lag and racket head acceleration - so I’m not sure how much this will help him. Even on clay - he destroyed clay court specialists like Ruud and had match points vs Alcaraz.

So I’m not completely sure if he needs this change.
