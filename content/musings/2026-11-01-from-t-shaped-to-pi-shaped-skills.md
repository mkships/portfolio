This is good advice in general. But a product manager in most companies is mostly cost centre.

You can argue in a few cases, where platform PM or tech PM can impact revenue but then so does his entire team of devs/designers in his product team.

Another way to look at is (and imo a better way) is earlier we used to have T shaped skill set - a software engineer with great design sense or a product manager who can also sell/do GTM or a designer who can write his own code.

Now with AI it become a Pi shaped skill set. One deep mastery with strong expertise in two or more dual skill tracks.
