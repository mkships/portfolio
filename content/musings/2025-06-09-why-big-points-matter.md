If the big points don’t matter - how come someone can win a match by actually winning fewer points than his opponent?

And something like this can truly only happen in tennis because of the point-game-set scoring system.

Tennis literally has names for all those points - game point, set point, break point, match point, championship point.

Yesterday itself Sinner won 1 point more than Alcaraz for the entire match - but he didn’t win the only three points that actually mattered in the match. Which are “BIG POINTS”.
